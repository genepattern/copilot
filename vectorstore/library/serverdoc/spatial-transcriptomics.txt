Spatial transcriptomics is an advanced technique for mapping gene expression within tissue architecture, revealing complex cellular interactions.
GenePattern's spatialGE modules provide a user-friendly, open-source solution for analyzing spatially-resolved transcriptomics data.
The spatialGE toolkit supports multiple platforms including GeoMx, Visium, and CosMx-SMI for comprehensive spatial gene expression analysis.
spatialGE.Preprocessing prepares raw spatial transcriptomics data through data ingestion, filtering, transformation, and pseudobulk operations.
spatialGE.STplot generates visualizations of gene expression, cluster memberships, and metadata within the spatial context of tissue samples.
spatialGE.SpatialAutocorrelation quantifies spatial uniformity of gene expression using statistical metrics like Moran's I and Geary's C.
spatialGE.STenrich identifies spatial expression patterns and tests for gene set aggregation within specific tissue regions.
spatialGE.STclust performs clustering analysis on spatial gene expression data to identify distinct cellular populations or tissue domains.
spatialGE.STdiff conducts differential expression analysis using both spatial and non-spatial statistical models.
spatialGE.STgradient analyzes gene expression gradients relative to reference tissue niches or domains.
These modules have been successfully applied to studying tumor microenvironments, particularly in melanoma and Merkel cell carcinoma research.
The toolkit aims to make complex spatial transcriptomics analysis accessible to researchers without advanced computational expertise.
