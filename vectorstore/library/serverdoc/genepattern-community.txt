GenePattern is a collaborative computational biology platform with multiple community interaction channels.
The GenePattern forum is a Google Groups-based discussion space for users to ask questions and share information about the platform.
Social media channels provide updates about new features, workshops, and community events.
A mailing list allows users to receive official announcements from the GenePattern team.
GenePattern Cloud is a web-based workspace supporting integrative genomics analysis with diverse bioinformatics tools.
The platform is a founding tool of the GenomeSpace initiative, facilitating data storage, analysis, and visualization.
Research papers associated with specific modules can be found in each module's documentation references.
Users can locate GenePattern-related publications through Google Scholar searches.
The platform maintains a comprehensive Code of Conduct emphasizing inclusivity and preventing harassment across all community spaces.
The Code of Conduct applies to online and offline interactions, including forums, repositories, and social media platforms.
Violations of the Code of Conduct may result in sanctions or expulsion from GenePattern community spaces.
The platform is committed to creating a harassment-free environment regardless of personal characteristics or identity.
