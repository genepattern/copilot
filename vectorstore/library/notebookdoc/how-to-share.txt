Collaborative project sharing in g2nb requires user authentication and explicit invitation mechanisms
Users can initiate project sharing by selecting the project's gear menu and choosing the "Share" option
The sharing interface allows entry of usernames or email addresses for potential collaborators
Multiple collaborators can be added by separating their contact information with commas or tabs
Shared project invitations are sent electronically to specified users
Recipients can view sharing invitations in the "Shared With Me" section of their g2nb Workspace
Invited users have the option to accept or reject project sharing invitations
Upon accepting an invitation, users gain view and edit access to the shared project
Accepted shared projects are accessible through the "Shared With Me" section of the g2nb Workspace
Project sharing facilitates collaborative research and analysis workflows between g2nb users
The sharing process requires both an initiator and recipient to be authenticated g2nb users
Shared projects maintain the original project structure and content during collaboration
