The g2nb community is a global collaborative network of researchers and developers in computational biology and bioinformatics.
The community's Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, both online and offline.
Community members are expected to be open to collaboration and receptive to constructive feedback.
The community values diversity and aims to create an inclusive environment where anyone can participate and contribute.
Members are encouraged to be considerate of each other's efforts and work towards the collective good of the community.
Respectful communication is a core principle, emphasizing courtesy and tact when discussing differing perspectives.
The community promotes mutual respect for individuals' positions, skills, commitments, and contributions.
g2nb software is open source and available under a BSD-style license, making it freely accessible to researchers.
The workspace is not intended for handling protected health information (PHI) or personally identifiable information (PII).
Community interactions should prioritize creating a positive, supportive, and collaborative environment.
Members are expected to work within established community processes and guidelines.
The code of conduct aims to foster a thriving, successful community through mutual understanding and respect.
