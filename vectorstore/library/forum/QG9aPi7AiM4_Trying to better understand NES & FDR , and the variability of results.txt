The GenePattern forum thread discusses the nuances of False Discovery Rate (FDR) and Normalized Enrichment Score (NES) in Gene Set Enrichment Analysis (GSEA), revealing that FDR is highly dependent on the entire gene set database context, and should not be interpreted in isolation or for individual gene sets; the recommended practice is to test custom signatures alongside larger gene set collections like C2.ALL, using NES and nominal p-value for evaluation when FDR becomes unreliable, with the ultimate validation being independent experimental confirmation rather than purely computational statistical manipulation.
