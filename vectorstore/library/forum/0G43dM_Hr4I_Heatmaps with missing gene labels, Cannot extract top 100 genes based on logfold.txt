A GenePattern user encountered issues with generating heatmaps: missing gene labels in microarray analysis, and difficulty extracting top 100 genes based on log fold change using the comparative marker selection and heatmap viewer modules; the recommended solution was to use the ComparativeMarkerSelectionViewer module and carefully review input files, with the caveat that extracting genes by rank works but extracting by fold change does not consistently produce the expected heatmap output.
