Amanda,


There are 2 possible causes but one is much more likely.  I suggest that you increase the memory for your job. IIRC MutSigCV defaults to 16GB so try again with 32 or 64GB.  Unfortunately some modules, typically those in R or MatLab (which MutSigCV is) do not give good error messages if they run out of memory.  Just to be safe you should also increase the walltime.  I have a test case running now (copying your job 508843) and will let you know and share the results if it works.


The other possible cause is when Amazon spot prices rise and preempt our GenePattern compute nodes.  This is pretty rare and usually affects pretty much all GP users for a short time when it happens.  There is no obvious sign of this so its unlikely in this case.


Hope this helps


Ted
