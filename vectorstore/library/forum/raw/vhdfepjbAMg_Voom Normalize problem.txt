Hello Shang-Yin,


There is trailing blank space on each of the lines in your gct file, causing the parser to determine that you have 15 samples rather than the 6 indicated by the header in the gct file.


I would suggest opening your file up in a text editor or IDE and making sure that all of your rows end after the final column.
