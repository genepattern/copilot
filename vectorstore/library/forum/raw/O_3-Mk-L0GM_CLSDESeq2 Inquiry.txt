Hi


I believe your interpretation is correct.  


My reasoning: 


The basename you provided (TCGA TumorATumorB Unstranded) is a red herring and what you need to look at is the class names that were autogenerated in the rest of the filenames which are all of the form "Tumor B vs Tumor A" .  In your cls file it has tumor A = 0, tumor B = 1. Looking at the GenePattern module source code for the write reports function, there is this comment


write.reports <- function(dds, output.file.base, class0, class1, fdrThreshold, topNCount, qc.plot.format) {
    # Note that this means we are comparing "class1 vs. class0" and thus class0 represents the control.


Finally looking at sample values of the first row of the *mean_values_by_class.txt" file we see (class names changed to protect your results)


id	classA classB
<row_id>	24.1018842175196	8591.81136580601



and in the report the log2FoldChange for the same <row id> is positive, to the FC must be classB/classA


Unfortunately I am not familiar enough with the DESeq2 calculations to work it all the way through and my simple calculations based on those mean values do not match exactly, but I am pretty sure the intepretation is correct.
