Hello Caroline, 


My apologies for the delay in replying.


I suspect you are running into an issue of lack of statistical power here.


GISTIC is a statistical method that requires at least 30 samples with good coverage to be accurate and function correctly. You have only 21 samples and in the job that failed, poor coverage. I suspect your other jobs with fewer than 30 samples completed because they have much better coverage. (A quick way to check is to load your seg file into IGV. If you see a lot of grey, you don't have enough coverage. Given your small sample set, it's also easy to see the low coverage from the seg count in your seg file. )



Please let us know if you need any other assistance.


Best
