I looked at job 1595922. The missing output files are not necessarily related to the traffic jam on the queue. I sent this along to the GSEA developer. Is there a reason you are using v18 instead of v19?

One workaround, for v18, is to explicitly set the "output.filename" instead of using the "<expression.dataset_basename>.zip" substitution.

BTW, the traffic jam does cause a very long wait time between when your job actually completes (a few minutes) and when it is reported as complete in GenePattern. Unfortunately there is no easy remedy for this. In the short term we will wait for the traffic to subside. If it is still sluggish, I will clear out the queue later this week.

Regards,
Peter
