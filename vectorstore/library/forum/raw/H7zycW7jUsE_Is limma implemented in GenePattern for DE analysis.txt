Hello Simone, 


The only limma module we have in GenePattern implements VOOM from the Bioconductor limma package - it's called 'VoomNormalize'


The NuGO GenePattern modules were created by an outside organization many years ago and hosted on their own servers, but not on ours. 


Please let us know if we can help in any other way.
