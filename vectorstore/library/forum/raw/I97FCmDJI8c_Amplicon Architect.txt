Hi Anne,


Thanks for reaching out with this bug report. The issue is related to the use of the advanced argument "AA extend mode": "VIRAL". This should currently remain as default ("EXPLORE"), even for samples that utilize the GRCh38_viral reference. The results will be the same for those two choices of extend mode, even on viral samples, in the vast, vast majority of cases. We will make a bugfix though for the issue you found in the next release.


Hopefully you can re-utilize the same BAM file generated earlier during the run when you re-load and re-run the job with the adjusted AA extend mode argument.


Thank you and please do not hesitate to reach out with more questions!
Jens
