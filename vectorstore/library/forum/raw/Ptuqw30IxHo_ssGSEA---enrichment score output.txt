Hi Yasmin,


Sorry for the delay.  We had to get an answer from the author who has moved on to a different institution and it took a while for the reply.  Here is his answer:
The output ssGSEA are not normalized and should be considered as measured in arbitrary units. For the purposes of downstream analysis e.g. matching the ssGSEA scores against a phenotype etc, they can be normalized or standardized.
I hope that helps!
