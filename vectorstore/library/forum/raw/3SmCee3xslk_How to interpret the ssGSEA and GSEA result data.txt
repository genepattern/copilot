Hi Shwet,
The primary results of GSEA are accessed through the "index.html" page which allow you to explore the enrichment of gene sets in each phenotype (i.e. Up or down regulated gene sets). The GSEA user guide has a section on Interpreting Results here: https://www.gsea-msigdb.org/gsea/doc/GSEAUserGuideTEXT.htm#_Interpreting_GSEA_Results its primarily focused on the Desktop application and not the GenePattern Module but the fundamentals are the same.

Additionally, you can use the EnrichmentMap add-on plugin in the desktop Cytoscape application to further visualize your enrichment results (https://enrichmentmap.readthedocs.io/en/latest/)


ssGSEA results are just in the results table (gct) and consist of the score for each set in each sample. If you have a CLS file that has sample phenotype information for the samples used in ssGSEA you can provide it and your results to either the ConstellationMap or ssGSEA_ROC modules which can help determine which gene sets are able to discriminate between those phenotypes, and in the case of ConstellationMap, can give you an overview of how similar the discriminative gene sets are to each other.



Let me know if you have further questions
