In a study I published with my colleagues (DOI: 10.1155/2018/5804376) , we were interested in investigating the expression signatures of target genes regulated by MYC in stomach cell lines.

We use a transcriptome and label the phenotypes as Control_siRNA versus MYC_gene.

The first is the normal transcriptome while the second is the transcript with the MYC silenced in the strains.

When we analyzed with GSEA Control_siRNA versus MYC_gene, we found several gene sets enriched for the Control_siRNA phenotype, which for us were upregulated genes.


  upregulated genes  =  Control_siRNA





downregulated genes  MYC_gene
