Hi Wira,

 

That is not accurate. The range of ssGSEA enrichment scores is not bounded to -1, +1. That is the case for the non-normalized enrichment score in the standard GSEA methodology, but ssGSEA uses a substantially different mathematical operation that isn’t constrained the same way.
