1) The green line is a q-value cut-off of 0.25.  Please note that q-values are not the same as p-values.  They are a measurement of false discovery rates of significant p values.  A q-value cut-off of 0.25 is used in many analyses.


2) The values on top are G scores.  An explanation of G scores is in Mermel et al. Genome Biology 2011.
