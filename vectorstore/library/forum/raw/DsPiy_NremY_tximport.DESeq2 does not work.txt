it looks like the input files you gave the last job are no longer on the server.  This can happen if they were uploaded on the run module page and the original job they are associated with has been purged which happens about 10 days after a job is run.


Try running again but upload fresh copies of all the input files.  To avoid this repeating in another 2 weeks you can upload them to your Files tab, and then use them from there.  The files on your "Files" tab are not purged unless you go over the 30GB storage quota.


Let me know if this works for you, if not I will dig deeper on the new job


Ted
