Hi,  


the file format definitions for the submap module are 


.gct :  https://www.genepattern.org/file-formats-guide#GCT
.cls : https://www.genepattern.org/file-formats-guide#CLS


From a quick look neither of your gct files  fit the format.  The first file (starting with S), on the second line there are additional spaces between the number of rows and columns.  It should just be #rows a single tab and then the # columns.


The second gct file (starting with i) is completely lacking the first 2 header lines required in a gct file.


Both files are missing a 'Description' column that should be present between the gene_id and the first column of values. Its not used in submap but must be present to be formatted properly. The easiest thing you could do here is just duplicate the gene_id column values, or even a column of blanks.


Hope this helps


Ted
