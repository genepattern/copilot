Hello,

 

This is not a scenario we've investigated in detail. Historically we've used untransformed TPM, and for other GSEA-family algorithms, we've not generally recommended log transformation, however, without extensive testing I can't give you a firm answer on what to do here.

 

Using the non-transformed TPM would probably be most in-line with other workflows that use ssGSEA, but depending on what you're trying to do with the results, it could be definitely worthwhile to run ssGSEA with both versions and do some comparisons. If you do so, we'd definitely be interested in hearing about your results.

 

Sorry I couldn't be of more help here,
