Hello Anjali,


Multiplot was deprecated and is no longer available on our hosted servers.


The author updated the previous suite of modules in 2014 as one module which, among other things, moved all of the preprocessing work into that same module.


It is available on our public server.


Please note, however, that while this is an update from the previous suite, it is still a legacy visualizer which requires the user of our VisualizerLauncher to view its output, as will be indicated on the Job Status page, after your job completes.


Please let us know if you have any questions or comments.


Best
-Barbara
