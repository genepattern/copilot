Sumithra,


I think you may be encountering 2 different errors.  First the job failed because the underlying AWS EC2 compute node shut down.  This probably has nothing to do with your inputs, it happens sometimes and is a side effect of GenePattern using the cheapest AWS resources available to keep our costs in check so that we can remain free to use.


Second when I rerun your job I get this error;


Error using gp_MutSigCV>MutSig_preprocess (line 302) missing mutation_type_dictionary_file Error in gp_MutSigCV (line 184
