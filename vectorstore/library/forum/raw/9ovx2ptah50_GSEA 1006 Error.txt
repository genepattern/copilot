Hi Maëlle,


Sorry for the delay in responding.  It's better to ask general GSEA questions over on the GSEA Forum as they are more likely to be seen by the GSEA team over there.  We may not be monitoring the GP forum as closely so it's easier to miss.


That said, I don't really have a good answer here.  It sounds like you got this to work in the end so maybe it's not important, but we could have a look at your data if you like to try to see what's going on.  I'm curious myself because this seems like a bug.


One possibility comes to mind, though.  Does your data happen to have a large number of NA or NaN values?


I happened to be looking at the CLS-loading code the other day for an unrelated reason and noticed there's some special case handling for this situation, so maybe it's related.  Unfortunately I can't articulate the conditions more clearly than that (not being the original author).  Seeing your data might help.


We definitely do recommend using gene_set permutation over phenotype permutation whenever possible, so let us know if you'd like us to take a look.  It might not be possible with your data, but I'm curious as I said.


- David
