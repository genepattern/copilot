Please refer to the Mathematical Appendix in the GSEA PNAS 2005 paper. Briefly, formula [1] there contains an exponent 'p'. When it is 0, the formula reduces to the classic scoring scheme (which has been described in the Mootha Nat Genet 2003 paper - which first introduced GSEA). When the exponent is 1, running enrichment score increments are weighted by the ranking metric values (e.g., log2FC or the like).
