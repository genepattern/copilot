Ravi,


we allow users to go past the 30GB limit for short periods of up to a week to handle cases like this.  Go ahead and upload the necessary files and run your analysis, then remove the inputs to drop back under the 30GB limit once you have successful runs.  You will see more aggressive warnings once you are >30GB but it will not immediately prevent you from running your analysis, though eventually that will happen if you stay over quota for too long, and/or the data might get deleted from the GenePattern servers.  Again, you should be OK for about a week.


Hope this helps


Ted
