Hi Seika,

Thanks for your interest in GSEA and GenePattern. One suggestion is that if
there are spaces or special characters in the names of files or certain other
items (gene set names, etc) it is possible that GSEA and/or GenePattern may get
confused about where to put the result. The workaround is to stick to naming
with alphanumeric characters as much as possible; you can use an underscore in
place of spaces, etc.

It's just a suggestion. If you have a job number on one of our public servers
we could check it out more closely.

Regards,
David
