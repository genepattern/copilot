Hi Nick,

 

I don’t think you’ll be able to get SNP results from this array regardless of what tool you use. The U133 array is a gene expression array, the arrays mentioned in the SNPFileCreator are specifically designed for detecting SNPs. There are methods for detecting copy number variants from expression arrays, but, to the best of my knowledge, expression arrays just don’t have the information needed to call SNPs.
