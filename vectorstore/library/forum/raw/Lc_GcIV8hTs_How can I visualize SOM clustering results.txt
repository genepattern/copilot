Hello,


The SOMClusterViewer is the correct visualizer for SOMClustering results.


Once it's run you will see a link to the page for our VisualizerLauncher which will walk you through installing that tool and allowing you to use the visualizer directly on your computer. 


This solves the security issue of allowing a browser to control your computer.


Please let us know if you have any difficulties.
