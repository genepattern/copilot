Hi Aurora,


It looks like you are trying to determine which sets of genes may be up-or downregulated between your 3-month and 6-month sample types. For that I would suggest you look at Gene Set Enrichment Analysis (GSEA) and the Molecular Signatures Database. You can read more about these at https://www.gsea-msigdb.org/gsea/index.jsp. The Molecular Signatures Database includes several collections of mouse gene lists that may be useful for your project. The GSEA method is available in GenePattern and also as separately downloadable software. 


Let me know if this is what you are looking for.


Thanks,
Michael
