1) How many samples are in your GISTIC input
2) The example that you show is a deletion of the entire Chromosome.  The point of GISTIC is to identify significantly reoccurring focal CN alterations.  A whole chromosome deletion is not a focal event.

That would explain it.
GISTIC is not supposed to pick up whole chromosome events, but there must be some sort of focal event being picked up on chromosome 2 which may be too small to see on the heatmap.
One thing you can try is adjusting the length of focal events to consider using the focal length cutoff parameter.
