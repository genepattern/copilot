Hello Sara, 


How are you supplying the input files? and in what browser?


Basically, the issue is that your input files are incorrect. They are bookmarks, rather than pointers to actual files.


Did you drag, copy & paste, or send the files from your HierarchicalClustering job?


Any of those should work, but it may be that your browser does not support one of those features correctly.


(hint - the input files should end with .cdt, .gtr or .atr once in the inptu file field. If it ends in .url, it won't work)
