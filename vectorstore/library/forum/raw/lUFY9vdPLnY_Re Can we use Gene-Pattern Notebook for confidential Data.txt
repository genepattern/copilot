Hello Vivek,


I am uncertain how confidential your data is, but the GenePattern Notebook Workspace isn't intended for use with Protected Health Information (PHI). If you feel that your data would fall under this category, we recommend that you download your own copy of GenePattern Notebook and run it on your computer. Instructions for doing this can be found at the link below:


https://gpnotebook-website-docs.readthedocs.io/en/latest/local-installation/


Thorin Tabor
GenePattern Team
