This is not supported in GenePattern. It's a nice feature request. To make it simple, I propose the following:
    re-define '_basename' to mean the filename up to but excluding the first '.'


I will discuss it with the rest of the GP Team and add it to our development radar.


FYI, it is not possible to use the ';' to separate commands. I run into this now and then and have to remind myself of the fact that the ';' is a bash idiom and does not match the general architecture of the GP server. In GenePattern each module equates to one and only one process aka command.


Thanks,
Peter
