Hello Pushpinder,


The del & amp_genes.conf_x files do not contain broad or focal data information, and those columns will also be blank in the all_lesions files for all versions of GISTIC_2.0.


See this post for more information: https://groups.google.com/a/broadinstitute.org/forum/#!topic/gistic-forum/3sjhSTnCHMo


I'm unfortunately unsure as to why your chromosomal locations are different, but I suspect it is because there is actually a mismatch between the GISTIC reference genome and the once used in creating the TCGA segmented data.


All the best
-Barbara
