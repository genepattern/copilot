Hi Laurie,

 

I ran some tests and It looks like the issue here is in the "Job Options" section, it would appear that with the default settings, this module doesn't get enough RAM to load in the index completely.

 

I'd recommend changing "job memory" to "32Gb", and perhaps also "job cpuCount" to "4". This should resolve the issue with HISAT2Aligner and allow your job to complete.

 

Also, from your screenshot it looks like you were able to get the Salmon.Quant job to complete, that's great to see!

 

Let us know if you encounter more issues
