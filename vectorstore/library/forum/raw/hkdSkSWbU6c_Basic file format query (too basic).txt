Hi Joe, 


There is no such thing as a "too basic" question!


We do have ARACNE https://genepattern.broadinstitute.org/gp/pages/index.jsf?lsid=urn:lsid:broad.mit.edu:cancer.software.genepattern.module.analysis:00090:2


I would suggest starting with the documentation for the module https://genepattern.broadinstitute.org/gp/module/doc/urn:lsid:broad.mit.edu:cancer.software.genepattern.module.analysis:00090:2


I would also suggest looking through our general proteomic offerings, either by searching for proteomics in the search bar in the panel on the left and/or by reading through an overview of our proteomics offerings on our webpage: http://software.broadinstitute.org/cancer/software/genepattern/proteomics


On that same webpage, you will also find user guides and tutorials, which may help you to figure out some of the things you are struggling with. That being said, please feel free to pester us as often as you like with any questions you have. We're very happy to help!


Best,
Barbara
