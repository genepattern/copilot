Hi Sruthi,


The file format is fine. The issue is that your job ran out of memory. If you click on the stderr.txt file you will see this error.


Reload the job, then scroll to the bottom of the job submit form, expand the Job Options, and increase the memory requested. I would suggest starting with 4 or 8GB - if you request more you will wait longer for your job to run, as there are more small memory hosts than large.


Please let us know if that resolves the issue.


Best
-Barbara
