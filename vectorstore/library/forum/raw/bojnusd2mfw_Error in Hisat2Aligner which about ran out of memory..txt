Hi


It looks like you requested only 1 CPU and 2 GB of ram for the jobs in the job options.  Try rerunning one of them with a bit more *see the job options section at the bottom of the job launch page) and once you find adequate resources then launch the others.  e.g. you could try 2-4 CPU and 8-16 Gb for your next pass.


Hope this helps,
