Hello Yajing,


The normalization is actually performed by the HierarchicalClustering module. HierarchicalClusteringImage is just a visual representation of the results of HierarchicalClustering.


To determine how this was achieved, I would suggest reading through the references as listed in the documentation for HierarchicalClustering. (Note also, that you should check to see if row normalization was performed, as that calculation is not run by default)


http://software.broadinstitute.org/cancer/software/genepattern/modules/docs/HierarchicalClustering/6
