Hi Hugo,


The ExpressionFileCreator module is the right choice for what you want to do. More detail on the analytic steps behind the RMA summarization method is at https://academic.oup.com/biostatistics/article/4/2/249/245074. 


The PreprocessDataset module applies to already summarized data and provides variation filters and post-summarization normalization options.
