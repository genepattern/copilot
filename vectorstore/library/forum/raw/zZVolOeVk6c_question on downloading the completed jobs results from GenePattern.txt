You can only download one job at a time.


If you are comfortable with shell programming, you can write a script to download the jobs via the REST API.
Here's curl command to download a single job.


Template:
    curl -u {user_id[:password]} https://genepattern.broadinstitute.org/gp/rest/v1/jobs/{job_id}/download > {job_id}.zip
Example:
    curl -u my_user https://genepattern.broadinstitute.org/gp/rest/v1/jobs/1/download > 1.zip


Here's one way to do this from a loop if your jobs happen to be in sequence.
Template:
  for job_id in $(seq -f "%.0f" $start_id $end_job_id);
      do curl -u my_user:my_pass https://genepattern.broadinstitute.org/gp/rest/v1/jobs/${job_id}/download > ${job_id}.zip; 
  done


Example:
  for job_id in $(seq -f "%.0f" 1 2);
      do curl -u my_user https://genepattern.broadinstitute.org/gp/rest/v1/jobs/${job_id}/download > ${job_id}.zip; 
  done



I tested this from the Mac OS X Terminal.app. Note: your job ids are not guaranteed to be in sequence; I leave it as an exercise for the reader to construct the loop for their particular batch of jobs.
