Miguel,


I'll look into letting a user configure the walltime in the next release. In the meantime, you can work around this issue by calling run_job with the wait_until_done parameter, and then polling periodically to see if the job has completed. An example of this is below:



submap_job = gpserver.run_job(submap_job_spec, wait_until_done=False)

while not submap_job.is_finished():           # Wait until the job is complete
    # print(submap_job.get_status_message())  # Other logic for checking the status of the job can also be added
    time.sleep(60)                            # Wait a minute and check the status again

output_list = submap_job.get_output_files()
