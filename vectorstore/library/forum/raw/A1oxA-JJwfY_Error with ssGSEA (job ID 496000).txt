Hai


it looks like the ssGSEA file parser is not happy with the number format in your gct file.  the '1,542' is the first appearance of a comma in your gct file. I did an experiment deleting all the commas (',') from your gct and the file runs fine.  So just do a quick find/replace in your favorite text editor replacing the commas with nothing and you should be good to go.


Hope this helps, 
Ted

Hai,


Just a quick note: those commas may represent decimal points, in which case you should replace with a '.' instead.  That is, '1,542' might mean '1542' as Ted suggested or it might mean '1.542'.  It will depend on how your dataset was constructed, so we can't advise you directly on that.


This is a localization difference, where ssGSEA is operating assuming US decimal formatting.
