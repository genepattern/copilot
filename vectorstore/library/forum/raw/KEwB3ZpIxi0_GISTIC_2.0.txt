I took a look at your seg file in IGV (you have to change the extenstion to .seg from .txt) and found that you have too little coverage and too few samples. 
You have only 15 samples and this is not sufficient for GISTIC. 
You need to have good coverage on all chromosomes for at least 30 samples in order for the algorithm to work and be accurate and function correctly.

You may find additional GISTIC specific assistance in the GISTIC help forum:  https://groups.google.com/a/broadinstitute.org/forum/#!forum/gistic-forum


All the best
