Hello, 


We reached out to Pablo Tamayo for his thoughts and this was his reply:


Yes you could if you e.g. map the gene identifiers into gene symbols first and then apply the submap approach.

However, the concordance of RNASeq RPKM and Microarray should be reasonable at the single gene level e.g. by looking at the overlapping gene symbols and rank normalizing the datasets. This will probably work even without having to resort to the submap approach.

Best,

--Pablo


To map the gene identifiers into gene symbols, you could use the module "CollapseDataset".


Please let us know if you have any other questions.
