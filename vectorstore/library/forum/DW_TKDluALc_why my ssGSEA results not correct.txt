User asked why their ssGSEA (single-sample Gene Set Enrichment Analysis) results do not fall between -1 and 1 when analyzing RNA-seq data normalized using CPM, TPM, and FPKM methods; expert Anthony Castanza clarified that ssGSEA enrichment scores are not mathematically constrained to the -1 to 1 range like standard GSEA, so the results appear reasonable and do not represent an error in the analysis, with the variation likely due to differences in gene set mapping between MSigDB versions and dataset collapse methods.
