A GenePattern user inquired about the orientation of fold change calculations in a DESeq2 module, specifically questioning whether a filename indicating "TumorATumorB" vs "TumorB vs TumorA" meant the fold change was reversed, with an expert confirming that the log2FoldChange would indeed be calculated as TumorB/TumorA, where positive values indicate higher expression in TumorB relative to TumorA.
