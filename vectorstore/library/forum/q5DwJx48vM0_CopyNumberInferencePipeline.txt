A GenePattern user encountered multiple issues while attempting to run the CopyNumberInferencePipeline module: initially experiencing a "module not installed" error for ParallelCBS, and subsequently encountering a problem where the non-optional parameter <input.file> was blank, which prevented the CopyNumberNoise step from executing. The support team acknowledged the issues, with Barbara Hill suggesting the first problem was resolved and indicating that the second error might relate to mismatched matrices or sample information files, though she could not provide a definitive solution.
