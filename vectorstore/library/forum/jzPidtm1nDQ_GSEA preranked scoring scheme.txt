GSEA preranked scoring thread summary: User inquired about differences between "classic" and "weighted" scoring schemes in GSEA pre-ranked analysis, with Arthur Liberzon clarifying that classic scoring uses no weighting (exponent 0), while weighted scoring incorporates ranking metric values (exponent 1), recommending consulting the GSEA 2005 PNAS paper's mathematical appendix for detailed explanation, and suggesting the user can choose metric based on specific research goals; also noted was the recommendation to use raw p-value rather than adjusted p-value when creating gene rankings.
