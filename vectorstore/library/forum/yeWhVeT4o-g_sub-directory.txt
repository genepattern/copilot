A GenePattern user experienced an issue when attempting to move a subdirectory within the "uploads" directory, which resulted in all files and directories disappearing from their profile. A technical support representative acknowledged a known bug in directory renaming/moving operations, restored the user's files (though with renamed files replacing special characters), but could not fully recover the original directory structure. The support recommended avoiding special characters and spaces in file/directory names to prevent future issues, and noted that a comprehensive fix was unlikely before June.
