Summary of GenePattern forum thread on MutSig2CV overlapping samples: A user encountered an issue with MutSig2CV flagging samples as duplicates despite having low mutation overlap (max 7%), and struggled to disable the duplicate patient removal feature using various parameter file configurations. The explanation provided suggests that MutSig2CV considers samples duplicates if they share 10+ mutations comprising at least 10% of their union, or 3+ mutations comprising ≥30% of their union. Potential causes include germline events, sequencing artifacts, or multiple biopsies from the same individual. No definitive solution was reached in this thread, with the user continuing to investigate the problem.
